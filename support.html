Before you email

For faster support, include the game name, device model, operating-system version, app version, a short description of what happened, and screenshots if useful. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Purchases

Purchases are generally processed by the store where you downloaded the game. Include the relevant store receipt or order identifier when asking us to investigate, but do not send full financial details.

Response time

We aim to review support messages promptly. Complex technical, privacy or payment-related requests can take longer while we investigate or verify necessary details.
